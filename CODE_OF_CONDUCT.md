# Code of Conduct

Be generous and honest. PythSol is an open-source project, you are a human being,
and we expect you to act the way you want to be treated.

Contribution, collaboration, and critique all happen here. If you see behaviour
that's unacceptable, contact the maintainers
([security](https://github.com/PythSol/pythsol/security/advisories)) and open a
private report. Every report is taken seriously and we'll respond within
two business days with either an explanation or a public statement.
